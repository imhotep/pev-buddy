Welcome to PEV Buddy

Turn-by-turn navigation for e-bikes, e-scooters & mopeds in San Francisco — from anywhere to anywhere.

  • Set a start and a destination: use your current location, search, or tap the map and choose Start here / Go here. Drag either pin to adjust.
  • Pick your vehicle type — routes and speed rules follow California law for that vehicle.
  • Map pins: chargers · BikeLink lockers · bike racks. Tap one to navigate there.
